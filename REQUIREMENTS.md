# Requirements

Standing constraints. Not tasks — a task gets closed, these do not. Anything here
holds until it is changed here.

Issues track work. CONTINUE.md says where the work stands. This says what must
stay true.

## Controls

**On a mouse the camera is driven the way Google Maps 3D is driven.** That is
what people already have in their hands, and anything else costs them the first
minute.

    drag                 pulls the ground about
    right-drag           swings round and tilts
    ctrl-drag            the same
    wheel                zooms at whatever is under the pointer, not at the
                         middle of the screen

**On a finger it is not.** A hand on glass is not a mouse, and the page is a
window rather than a map: a man on a bluff turns his head, he does not slide the
bluff.

    one finger           turns and tilts the view
    two fingers          pinch to zoom, drag to pull the ground about

Maps' own one-finger pan was here and it cost the left half of the screen to a
thumb stick, because a pan and a turn cannot both live on one finger. Turning is
the one worth having.

**Nothing is drawn over the view that a gesture can do instead.** The stick that
is left belongs to the modes that drive something, where a thumb has to hold a
throttle open.

**A swing turns around the ground in the middle of the screen**, not around
whatever point the camera was last left orbiting. Turning around a point out on
the water throws the whole world past instead of showing the other side of what
you are looking at.

No limit on how close the camera may come to what it is looking at.

## What is drawn over the view

**How you are getting about is always on screen and always in reach.** It sits
in the bottom right, at a thumb's size, and it does not go behind the hamburger
with the readouts. Everything drawn over the view fades when it has not been
touched, and the tap that brings it back is the same tap that presses it.

## The cabin at 389 W Bluff Rd

**It is drawn in the best detail the measurements support.** The lot is the point
of the page and the cabin is the point of the lot. Where the lidar, the county
record or a photograph settles something, that is what is drawn, and where
nothing settles it, that is said out loud rather than filled in.
